A LITTLE MORE TO KNOW

Curious?
Good.

How do I place an order?

Open a product, pick your colour and quantity, and select “Request on WhatsApp”. Review the prepared message and send it to TDi Labs. Your request becomes an order only after we confirm the price, availability, delivery, and payment details together.

Can you print my own design?

Use the custom request form to describe your idea. Share a reference link, then send your images or model files in WhatsApp. We'll review printability, material options, and sizing before providing a quote.

Will my print look exactly like the preview?

The objects shown here are interactive 3D illustrations. Actual filament colours, dimensions, texture, and layer lines can vary. Ask us for a real photo or a colour sample before confirming your order.

What about pricing and delivery?

Final pricing depends on size, material, quantity, and customisation. Delivery charges and lead times are confirmed directly in WhatsApp. This preview catalogue uses illustrative products and sample prices.
